Dr. LeAnna R. Wilson

Turning Strategy Into Systems That Scale

Twenty years ago, I began my career as a K-12 classroom teacher. I learned in those early years that, as challenging as the job was, I wasn’t on my own and that the typical teacher occupies an important space in a hierarchy of professionals and educational leaders whose job it is to provide them with the tools - and imbue them with the desire - to be as effective as possible. My experience as a learner from those educational leaders gave me the impetus to broaden the scope of my impact, and I sought to become one of those professionals.

The trajectory of my career since then has led me to occupy executive positions at Pearson and Student Success Agency, where I cultivated partnerships and created strategies that provided operational frameworks, leadership coaching, employee and client oversight, and data-informed outcomes that lead to sustainable impact.

Across every chapter of my career, the throughline has been consistent: build the systems, teams, and partnerships that turn strategy into measurable results at scale. I bring a relationship-centered leadership approach grounded in data, operational discipline, and cross-functional alignment. I believe strong relationships create opportunities, but sustainable outcomes mostly happen when those relationships are supported by the right systems, clear accountability, and teams equipped to execute.

Today, my mission continues to be to help organizations translate vision and strategy into scalable, sustainable impact.

Instructional Systems / Online Learning

Dufour PLC Model for Virtual Teacher Communities

The Dufour Professional Learning Communities model was built for “brick-and-mortar” teachers: those who relate to each other and instruct their students in person. I led the project at Pearson that adapted the Dufour model for 20+ PLC Department Chairs and hundreds of online teachers for a fully online format, one that allowed virtual teacher communities to collaborate, evaluate practices, and improve instruction with the same effectiveness as those “brick-and-mortar” teachers.

Risk & Retention Analytics

Partner Health Score Tracker

Always aware of the importance of predicting implementation success and retention of clients, I built the Partner Health Score Tracker to quantify partner health across all 23 districts and grant sources, identifying early warning signs before they became renewal-cycle surprises. Built through Notion and by pulling weekly data from Metabase, the Partner Health Score Tracker became the tool the team used for intervening earlier rather than reacting later, turning partner management from a reactive stance to a proactive one.
